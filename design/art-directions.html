Seven skins for one card table

Pick a direction and the table restyles live. Tap cards in your hand to lift them. Drag the width slider down to phone size (about 48 px) to see which looks stay readable, because that is where most card-game art fails. All card art and patterns here are original CSS and SVG.

My recommendation

Push back first

  • Art won't beat them on traffic. The big incumbent card sites win on search: one page per game, rules text under the table, many languages, years of backlinks [INFERRED]. A nicer skin helps people stay and share, but it won't bring them in.
  • Plain is a feature. Their flat green-felt look reads at any size and loads fast [KNOWN, MED, from memory; not re-checked]. Every direction here costs legibility or speed somewhere.

What I'd build

  • Engine default: Miðnætursól. Most legible, cheapest, and themable through tokens.
  • Identity: Baðstofa. The Icelandic angle is the one thing a generic clone can't copy. Use it for the logo, menus, card backs and win screens, and keep its card faces close to Miðnætursól's indices.
  • Later, as unlockable skins: Handrit, Norðurljós, Riso, Pixel. They come almost free once every colour and font is a token.

Rules for any skin

  • Below about 64 px card width, switch to compact faces: a big index plus a big suit, no pips.
  • Offer four-colour suits. Red/black alone fails for some colour-blind players [COMMON].
  • Card faces as one SVG sprite or CSS, not 52 bitmaps × N skins.
  • Keep animation to deal, play and sweep. Glow and blur effects cost frames on low-end phones [COMMON].
