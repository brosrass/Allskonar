Pips Recorder

Records a browser tab or window to a video file on this computer. Use it to capture a few hands of a game, then upload the video and the markers file to Claude.

Ready
    Tips for a useful recording
    1. Choose Chrome Tab in the share dialog and pick the game tab. Tab capture is sharpest and hides the rest of your screen.
    2. Play 3 to 5 full hands at normal game speed. Don't rush the computer's moves.
    3. Add a marker (M while this page has focus, or the button) at moments worth a closer look.
    4. Keep each video under about 5 minutes; several short clips beat one long one.
